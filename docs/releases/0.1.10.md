## Fixes

- Mark subscription rewards as **Subscription required** and prevent them from being queued.
- Remove queued campaigns when they have no unclaimed watch-time rewards left, without triggering sleep or a queue-complete notification.
- Keep campaigns with remaining watch-time rewards available to queue, even when they also contain subscription rewards.
- Show subscription requirements in campaign details and inventory instead of zero-minute watch progress.

## Install or update

Download **Dropfarmer_0.1.10_x64-setup.exe** below, or use **Settings → App updates** in an existing installation. Your saved login, queue, and settings are kept. Farming stays paused after updating.

Refresh campaigns after updating to load subscription requirements and remove ineligible queue entries.

Windows x64.
