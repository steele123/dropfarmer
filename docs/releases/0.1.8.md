## Fixes

- Renew Twitch browser sessions in the background without opening a browser window, so renewal does not take focus from a game.
- Keep normal browser sign-in visible when you choose to reconnect. Failed background renewal does not open a visible window as a fallback.

## Install or update

Download **Dropfarmer_0.1.8_x64-setup.exe** below, or use **Settings → App updates** in an existing installation. Your saved login, queue, and settings are kept. Farming stays paused after updating.

Windows x64.
