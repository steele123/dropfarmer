## What's new

- Analytics: claimed drops, completed campaigns, active farming time, and totals by game.
- Saved reward history for each Twitch account, with search and game filters. Rewards stay in history after their campaigns disappear from Twitch.
- CSV export for all rewards matching your history filters, saved to Downloads.
- Expiry warnings for rewards ending within 24 hours, expired reward windows, and rewards with too little time left for their remaining progress and prerequisites.
- A Move to top button for queued campaigns.
- A desktop notification when minimizing or closing to the system tray.

Farming time starts being tracked with this version. Existing claims are imported when Twitch returns them; history dates show when Dropfarmer first recorded each claim. Time and expiry estimates do not guarantee progress or stream availability.

## Install or update

Download **Dropfarmer_0.1.6_x64-setup.exe** below, or use **Settings → App updates** in an existing installation. Your saved login, queue, and settings are kept. Farming stays paused after updating.

Windows x64. Windows notification settings can suppress tray notices.
