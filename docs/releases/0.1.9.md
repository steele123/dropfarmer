## Fixes

- Read Twitch's received-rewards list so completed drops show as claimed even after they leave the in-progress list.
- Backfill missing reward history, including rewards whose campaigns are no longer listed.
- Preserve confirmed claims and farming-time totals without duplicating rewards on later syncs.
- Show Twitch's award dates when available and include them in CSV exports.

## Install or update

Download **Dropfarmer_0.1.9_x64-setup.exe** below, or use **Settings → App updates** in an existing installation. Your saved login, queue, and settings are kept. Farming stays paused after updating.

Refresh campaigns after updating to import your received rewards and correct campaign progress.

Windows x64.
