## Fixes

- Renew expiring Twitch browser sessions automatically using the saved login.
- Restore saved logins even when their short-lived browser proof has expired.
- Keep the queue in place during renewal, and retry temporary renewal failures without repeatedly asking for sign-in.

A separate browser window may briefly open during renewal and closes when Twitch confirms access. If Twitch revokes your login, you still need to reconnect.

## Install or update

Download **Dropfarmer_0.1.7_x64-setup.exe** below, or use **Settings → App updates** in an existing installation. Your saved login, queue, and settings are kept. Farming stays paused after updating.

Windows x64.
