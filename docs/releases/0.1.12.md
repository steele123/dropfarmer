## Added

- **My queue** now shows remaining watch time and an estimated finish time. When farming is paused, it shows when the queue could finish if started now.
- Each queued campaign shows its remaining watch time. Warnings flag rewards that may miss their deadline in the current queue order.
- Estimates account for current progress, prerequisite rewards, shared watch time, and scheduled starts. Missing data, reconnects, offline channels, and deadline risks show an explanation instead of a finish time.

## Changed

- **Auto farm** has its own tab beside **My queue**, with followed games and the on/off switch moved out of Settings. The **Manage** shortcut opens the new tab.

## Install or update

Download **Dropfarmer_0.1.12_x64-setup.exe** below, or use **Settings → App updates**. Your saved login, queue, and settings are kept.

Finish times assume eligible streams stay live and rewards are claimed promptly. New campaigns added by Auto farm can extend the estimate.

Windows x64.
