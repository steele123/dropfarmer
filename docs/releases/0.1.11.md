## Added

- Follow games in **Settings → Auto farm followed games**. While enabled, Dropfarmer checks every 15 minutes and queues new eligible watch-time drops, including while the app is in the tray.
- Follow or unfollow a game from campaign details. New campaigns go at the end of your queue; subscription rewards, claimed drops, and campaigns requiring account linking are skipped.
- A first-use tray notice shows where to find Dropfarmer and how to reopen or quit it. Choose **Move to tray**, **Keep open**, or remember your choice with **Don’t show this again**.
- Clearer desktop notifications when Dropfarmer moves to the tray, including when minimized through Windows.

## Auto farm

The first check includes current eligible drops for your followed games. Pausing farming also turns off Auto farm. Sleep when finished is unavailable while Auto farm is enabled. The app must stay running to check for new drops.

## Install or update

Download **Dropfarmer_0.1.11_x64-setup.exe** below, or use **Settings → App updates** in an existing installation. Your saved login, queue, and settings are kept. Auto farm starts turned off; choose your games and enable it in Settings.

Windows x64.
